    Threat Vector Analysis

    Our pre-trained XGBoost ensemble models indicate specific high-risk transactional vectors. The most prominent indicators of fraud are:

    • Geographical Disparity: Transactions executed at merchants where Euclidean distance exceeds 1.5 units (approx. 100+ miles) from the cardholder's zip coordinates show a 93.4% high-risk correlation.
    • Nighttime Spikes: Card actions recorded between 10:00 PM and 3:00 AM local time have a significantly higher incidence of fraudulent alerts, specifically in the shopping_net and misc_net categories.
    • High Amount Anomalies: Amounts exceeding $500.00 that deviate significantly from a user's normal historical patterns undergo severe standard-scaling penalties, resulting in high predictive probabilities.
